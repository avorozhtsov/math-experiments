Construct finite fields F_25, F_49, F_125, and in general fields of size p^k.
Prove the existence of fields of size p^k for prime p greater than 2.
The proof reduces to the existence of irreducible polynomials of degree k with coefficients from the field F_p.
The number is described by polynomials whose coefficients are the Möbius function.

We also prove that any field has a size of p^k.
