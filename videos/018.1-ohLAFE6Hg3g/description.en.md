This time, while studying partial sums of prime powers, we discovered the first zero of the zeta function.

The "prime number noise", which we found in the previous video, turns out to consist of several components, including sine waves. The main sine wave has the form a * sin(14.1347... log(x) + b), where the number 14.1347... is the imaginary part of the first zero of the zeta function.
