In this video, we will discover that Bernoulli polynomials are a special case of the Euler-Maclaurin formula. We will go through the derivation of Bernoulli polynomials and observe the connection between the coefficients in these polynomials and the analytic continuation of the sum of reciprocals of s-th powers of natural numbers. This continuation is called the Zeta function ζ(z).
