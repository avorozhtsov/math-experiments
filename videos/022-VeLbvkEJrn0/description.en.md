Today, from simple observations about the powers of prime numbers in Catalan numbers, we will arrive at Chebyshev's inequalities:

A * n/log(n) ≤ π(n) ≤ B * n / log(n)

where π(n) is the number of prime numbers less than or equal to n.

We will create beautiful fractal graphs of the powers of prime numbers in the product of the first n Catalan numbers. We will also explore generating functions of frequency triangles.

Continuing our study of Wolfram Mathematica, we will actively use functions like Series, Accumulate, and Tally.
