When studying the Legendre symbol, questions arise about possible extensions of this function to non-prime values of the second argument. In this video, we will conduct several simple mathematical experiments, obtain a series of interesting results, and... consider an extension of the Legendre symbol that is a periodic function in both arguments.
Periodicity in the second argument is a very unexpected thing. This brings us closer to the first law of reciprocity—the quadratic law of reciprocity.
